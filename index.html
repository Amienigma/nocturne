Presets only load into the section you are editing when you tap one.

Sections A–D are four patterns (fresh sessions start with 2-bar classic 808 trap at 145 BPM: A TRAP, B RAGE, C DARK breakdown, D FILL). Every section has its own looper: its ▶ PLAY loops just that section (½, 1 or 2 bars); tap other sections' ▶ PLAY while it runs and they join in sync at the next bar, layered; tap a playing section to stop it. ■ STOP ALL (top bar) stops everything. ▶ SONG plays the chain in order and loops it; build it with +A–D, ⌫ removes the last part, CLEAR empties it, and tapping a chain chip changes that part. Switching between section loopers and the song never stops the beat. Tap a section letter to edit it; LOAD CLASSIC 808 brings the classic sections back (undoable). Tap a step, then use the step bar for velocity, rolls (1/32 to 1/128 and triplet runs, with ramps), chance, length and 808 slide (glides from the previous note). Select a track, pick a note or chord, then tap steps. On a phone you see one track at a time: swipe or use ◀ ▶ to switch ([ and ] on a keyboard). Hold, right-click or shift-click a step to cycle its velocity (40 → 60 → 80 → 100%); brighter = louder. Everything autosaves; CLEAR, RANDOMIZE and presets can be undone. 808 and chord tracks store a pitch per step.

WRITE puts the progression on the Synth track of the section you're editing, using the selected synth. Every note stays in the key and scale. Arps play one chord note per step. With 808 FOLLOWS THE ROOT on, existing 808 notes move to each chord's root (to the nearest octave), and an empty 808 track gets one root note per chord. Undo reverts it. Synth edits are saved with the beat.

MIXER VOLUME · PAN · REVERB · DELAY PER TRACK

Reverb and delay are shared returns (one of each, created only when a send is up). Pads and keys you play live skip the mixer.

TAPE DECK PLAY · LOOP · STOP · REWIND PER LAYER
Changes land on the next bar line.

Every track is a cassette layer. ▶ plays it in sync with the beat, ⟲ loops it on its own over the length you pick (¼ bar to 2 bars, or custom steps), so a 1-bar hat loop can ride over a 2-bar 808 line. ■ stops that layer, ◀◀ rewinds it to the top of its loop. While the beat plays, each change waits for the next bar so nothing falls out of time; ▶ ALL IN SYNC brings every layer back together. Mute / solo, sections and song mode still apply. TAPE (top bar) adds wow, flutter, warm saturation and hiss.
